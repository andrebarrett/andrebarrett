## AI security architecture for the age of AI agents

[![AI Security Architects](/img/ai-security/ai-security-architects.png)](https://aisecurityarchitects.com)

AI is being wired into every layer of the enterprise, from customer-facing chat to autonomous agents with access to production systems. Most security programs were not designed for this.

I lead architecture for an AI gateway and agentic platform in a regulated industry, working alongside security, risk and compliance teams to take AI applications safely into production. I founded **AI Security Architects** to share practical, vendor-neutral guidance with the people doing the same.

## Areas of focus

### Secure AI architecture
Reference architectures for LLM applications, RAG pipelines, MCP gateways and agentic workflows: identity, data boundaries, guardrails and isolation.

### AI threat modeling
Prompt injection, data poisoning, model supply chain and tool abuse, mapped to controls architects can actually deploy.

### AI governance and strategy
Turning frameworks like NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act into architecture decisions and roadmaps.
