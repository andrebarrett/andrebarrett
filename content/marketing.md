## Great technology still needs a brand that sells

When starting a business, most people think of a brand as a name and a logo. They create these, launch, and then wonder why they struggle to make sales. I have seen products that were a technical success while the startups behind them failed.

That is why I treat marketing as part of building a product. With my consultancy I launched digital products of our own as case studies, to learn what makes a launch succeed. One trait stood out in everything that worked: a strong brand. So I wrote a book about how to create one.
