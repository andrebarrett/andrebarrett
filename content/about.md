## I'm Andre Barrett. I live in London, where I write software and build tech businesses.

I have loved writing software ever since I wrote my first few lines of executable code when I was 10 years old. One of my fondest memories is of the first computer I ever owned: the Sinclair ZX Spectrum (which coincidentally is as old as I am).

My dream growing up was to be an inventor. I soon found out that the only thing I loved as much as computers was being an entrepreneur. It turned out to be the perfect pairing: I could create things for people and sell them. Looking back, starting my software consultancy, iMobilize, was inevitable.

Many of the products I built for clients were a technical success, yet the startups behind them still failed. So I set out to understand why. What I learned about strategy, branding, marketing, finance and leadership now shapes everything I do.
