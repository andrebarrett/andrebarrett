## Software that gives a business an edge

I have spent over 20 years building software, from mobile apps used by millions to enterprise cloud and AI platforms. I work across the whole life of a product, from the first business requirement through to production.

Since 2010 I have run **iMobilize Ltd**, a London software consultancy. We build custom mobile apps and web platforms, backed by impartial, strategic and practical advice.

## How we deliver

1. **Strategy.** Briefings to capture requirements and constraints, then product design.
2. **Design.** Sketches, mock-ups and wireframes informed by best practice and competitor products.
3. **Development.** Agile, data-driven delivery using Scrum and test-driven development.
4. **Launch.** Pre-launch awareness on social media and follow-up after release.
5. **Life-cycle management.** Managing the product from inception through to service and retirement.
