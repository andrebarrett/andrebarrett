## Turn your coding skills into a profitable software business

![Software Startup Academy, founded by Andre Barrett](/img/academy/software-startup-academy.jpg)

Great code doesn't build a business on its own. As developers we're trained to solve problems logically. Running a profitable business takes a different set of skills: managing resources, marketing, selling and leading people. Nobody shows us how.

I founded **Software Startup Academy** to teach developers and tech founders the strategy, marketing and systems to launch and grow a software business.

## The method

1. **Clarity.** Diagnose where you really are, so you work on the one thing that moves your business forward.
2. **Skills.** Step-by-step training in strategy, marketing, sales, leadership, productivity and finance.
3. **Action.** Practical actions, templates and mentoring that increase your business effectiveness.
